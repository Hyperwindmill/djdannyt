Who

Open format, strong roots.

I'm Daniele. I started as a b-boy: dancing, teaching, and making the mixes the hip hop and breaking classes trained to, on a PC, the rough way. By 2009 I had my first console and was DJing the breaking trainings at the Buridda and the hip hop jams. The name came from the scene. I kept it.

Genova nights after that: hip hop and dancehall rooms, commercial open-format sets that ran six hours without a repeat. At a scene night there is always a cypher, and I DJ it the way I used to dance in it: the right BPMs, breaks stretched by hand, eyes on the circle.

Guitar since I was six, classical then electric, a short life in a metal band. That is why distortion, melody and groove all feel like home when I produce.

On AI, plainly: my first album, Uncertain Identity, I wrote and directed, and an AI generated it. It says so on every platform. Since September 2026 I do it the other way round: hand-made in Ableton, my own voice included, AI only when a track asks for it. Either way, it is on the label.

DJ Danny-T is a DJ and producer from Genova, Italy. B-boy by origin, he DJed the city's breaking trainings, jams and clubs from 2009 to 2013, and came back to the decks in 2026 after a long break. Open format by trade: dancehall, dubstep, techno, hip hop. Since 2026 he also releases his own music, hand-made in Ableton or made with AI, declared either way.

Live

Then, a break, now.

2009 to 2013: the breaking trainings at the Buridda, the jams, the AQA residency, Friday opener and full Saturdays. Then a long break: day job, marriage, a family. Then I got back to work.

The comeback: DJ Kamo's open-turntable night in the Genova vicoli, September 2026, on the decks from six until close. Records of my own since August 2026. I show up with vinyl and a laptop, plug into whatever is on the table, and stay until the lights.

What I am good for: scene nights (hip hop, dancehall, the cypher), warm-up to peak in open-format rooms, breaking jams and battles, club techno and bass sets built around my own records.

Tech rider

Small rider, no drama.

I bring

  • Pioneer DDJ-SB controller — the whole rig when travelling light
  • Optionally a Numark V7 motorised deck on top
  • Laptop with VirtualDJ and Serato, full library offline
  • Own audio interface for DVS: plugs into any mixer, with vinyl
  • Headphones, cables, phone hotspot as backup network

Or I use yours

  • Any mixer with two free channels (line or phono) for the DVS setup
  • CDJs and similar: fine — a rekordbox USB stick or the laptop hooked up, just say which in advance
  • Turntables welcome, not required

Always needed

  • A stable table and one power socket
  • A booth monitor
  • 15 minutes of soundcheck before doors
  • A microphone on the mixer for the cypher
